# iverif.io Platform Study Guide: AI-Powered Document Validation

This study guide provides a comprehensive overview of the iverif.io platform, a RegTech SaaS solution designed specifically for EU energy subsidy operators. It synthesizes the core themes of market positioning, technical benchmarks, and the brand's strategic identity to facilitate a deep understanding of the platform's role in the energy subsidy ecosystem.

---

## I. Key Concepts

### 1. The Core Problem and Market Opportunity
The EU energy subsidy market is currently characterized by high regulatory complexity. Dossiers often consist of 10–20 documents that must be cross-checked for consistency. Manual review is time-consuming and prone to human error, often resulting in rejection discovered only after submission. This leads to lost subsidy claims and high costs for correction.

### 2. Product Definition: The iverif.io Platform
The platform is an AI-powered document validation engine that performs real-time cross-document field extraction and rule checking. It is designed to act as a "Signal in the System," transforming regulatory complexity into a high-precision instrument panel where every action is traceable and verifiable.

**Technical Benchmarks:**
*   **Processing Speed:** Under 30 seconds per full dossier.
*   **Accuracy Target:** Less than 2% rejection rate.
*   **Capacity:** Unlimited document types (invoices, attestations, technical sheets, etc.).
*   **Security:** EU-hosted infrastructure and GDPR compliant.

### 3. Competitive Differentiation
Unlike generic OCR tools or legacy Document Management Systems (DMS) like SharePoint, iverif.io provides:
*   **Cross-Document Reasoning:** Generative AI that understands relationships between different documents in a dossier.
*   **Pre-configured Rules:** Native support for six major EU programmes (CEE, Conto Termico, BEG, ECO4, Primes Énergie, and MOVES III).
*   **Managed Onboarding:** A dedicated team configures specific rules for the user, allowing a "go-live" period of 5–7 days.

### 4. The Compliance Operator Persona
The target audience, referred to as "The Compliance Operator," includes Back-office Managers, Compliance Officers, and Operations Directors. Their primary motivations are:
*   **Primary:** Regulatory peace of mind.
*   **Secondary:** Operational pride in high-precision work.
*   **Tertiary:** Reclaiming time lost to manual tasks.

## IV. Glossary of Important Terms

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Audit Trail** | A complete, timestamped log of every validation check, ensuring regulatory compliance and accountability during audits. |
| **BEG** | *Bundesförderung für effiziente Gebäude*; a German federal funding programme for efficient buildings. |
| **CEE** | *Certificats d'Économie d'Énergie*; a French energy savings certificate programme. |
| **Cross-Document Reasoning** | The ability of an AI engine to compare data across different documents (e.g., matching an invoice to a technical sheet) to identify inconsistencies. |
| **Dossier** | A collection of 10–20 documents (invoices, certificates, affidavits) required to claim a single energy subsidy. |
| **ECO4** | The fourth stage of the Energy Company Obligation scheme in the UK, focusing on energy efficiency. |
| **Managed Onboarding** | A service model where a specialized team configures the software's validation rules for the client, rather than leaving the client to self-serve. |
| **Point of Difference (POD)** | Characteristics that make iverif.io unique, such as pre-configured EU rules and 5–7 day implementation. |
| **RegTech** | "Regulatory Technology"; a class of SaaS designed to help companies comply with regulations efficiently. |
| **Signal Green (#2EE600)** | The primary brand color used for call-to-action (CTA) buttons and validation indicators. |
| **Validation Engine** | The core technical component of iverif.io that extracts data and applies programme rules in real-time. |
