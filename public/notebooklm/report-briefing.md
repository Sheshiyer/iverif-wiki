# iverif.io Brand Intelligence Report: Strategic Briefing

## 1. Executive Summary

iverif.io is a specialized RegTech SaaS solution engineered to provide a high-precision validation layer for the EU energy subsidy market. The platform addresses a critical systemic failure: the high volume of lost subsidy claims resulting from avoidable document inconsistencies. By deploying an AI-powered validation engine, iverif.io allows operators to verify every field, document, and program-specific rule before submission, transforming the traditional manual workflow into a verifiable, automated process.

### Launch Snapshot
*   **Core Problem:** Avoidable document errors and data inconsistencies leading to rejected claims and high post-submission correction costs.
*   **Solution:** An AI-driven validation engine providing real-time extraction, cross-document field reconciliation, and explainable AI output.
*   **Primary Role:** Delivering regulatory precision and operational risk mitigation for complex subsidy dossiers.

### Emotional Drivers
*   **Regulatory Peace of Mind:** Absolute assurance that subsidy claims will not be invalidated due to clerical or compliance errors.
*   **Operational Pride:** The professional satisfaction of validating dossiers with a level of precision that matches the complexity of the work.
*   **Time Reclaimed:** Systematic conversion of manual, hour-long cross-checking tasks into 30-second automated validations.

## 5. Product Overview & Technical Benchmarks

### Product Definition
The **Validation Engine** is a configurable rule-based system designed for real-time field extraction and inconsistency flagging. It utilizes Gen-AI to reason across dossiers, ensuring that data in an invoice matches the data in an installation certificate, with full transparency provided via explainable AI output.

### Technical Benchmarks
*   **Processing Speed:** 30 seconds per full dossier.
*   **Accuracy Target:** <2% rejection rate.
*   **Document Types:** **Unlimited** (Invoices, attestations, certificates, technical sheets, affidavits, etc.).
*   **Integration:** **Full API** for CRM, DMS, and workflow integration.
*   **Infrastructure:** **EU-hosted** and fully GDPR compliant.
*   **Onboarding Timeline:** 5–7 days for full system go-live.

### Programme Coverage
Pre-configured logic is currently available for:
1.  **CEE** (Certificats d'Économie d'Énergie)
2.  **Conto Termico**
3.  **BEG** (Bundesförderung für effiziente Gebäude)
4.  **ECO4**
5.  **Primes Énergie**
6.  **MOVES III**
