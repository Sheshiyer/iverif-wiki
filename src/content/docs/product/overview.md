# Product Overview

## What is iverif.io?

iverif.io is an AI-powered document validation platform specifically designed for EU energy subsidy operators. It transforms the manual, error-prone process of checking subsidy dossiers into a streamlined, automated workflow with pre-configured programme rules and complete audit trails.

---

## The Problem

Energy subsidy dossiers require cross-checking 10-20 supporting documents against programme-specific rules. Manual validation is:
- **Slow** — hours per dossier, creating backlogs
- **Error-prone** — human oversight leads to rejections and compliance risks
- **Unscalable** — volume spikes overwhelm teams
- **Opaque** — no audit trail for regulatory bodies

---

## The Solution

iverif.io automates document validation with AI trained on EU energy subsidy regulations:

| Capability | Impact |
|------------|--------|
| AI-powered document validation | Reduces errors by up to 90% compared to manual checking |
| Pre-configured programme rules | Works out of the box for CEE, BEG, ECO4, and other major programmes |
| Complete audit trail | Every validation check timestamped and logged for regulatory compliance |
| Managed onboarding | Dedicated support ensures operators process real dossiers within one week |

---

## Target Users

- **Energy subsidy operators** processing CEE, BEG, ECO4, and other EU energy subsidy programmes
- **Regulatory bodies** auditing submitted dossiers for compliance
- **Compliance teams** ensuring documentation meets programme-specific requirements

---

## Key Differentiators

1. **Domain-specific** — Built exclusively for EU energy subsidy programmes, not generic document processing
2. **Pre-configured rules** — No months-long setup; programme logic embedded from day one
3. **Managed expertise** — Human-in-the-loop support, not just software
4. **Audit-ready** — Complete timestamped logs for regulatory scrutiny

---

## Business Impact

- **Faster processing** — Dossiers validated in minutes, not hours
- **Higher approval rates** — Fewer rejections due to documentation errors
- **Regulatory confidence** — Complete audit trails demonstrate compliance
- **Scalable operations** — Handle volume spikes without proportional staff increases
