# Secondary Personas

## Regulatory Auditor

**Role:** Government or programme administrator auditing subsidy dossiers
**Primary concern:** Verifying compliance and detecting fraud
**Pain point:** Reviewing thousands of dossiers manually with limited time
**How iverif.io helps:** Complete audit trails, standardized validation reports, flagged anomalies

---

## Compliance Director

**Role:** C-level or VP responsible for regulatory compliance across programmes
**Primary concern:** Risk management and audit readiness
**Pain point:** Lack of visibility into validation accuracy and error trends
**How iverif.io helps:** Real-time dashboards, error trend analysis, compliance reporting

---

## IT Director

**Role:** Technology decision-maker evaluating integration and security
**Primary concern:** Data security, system reliability, integration complexity
**Pain point:** SaaS solutions that require months of integration work
**How iverif.io helps:** REST API, SAML/SSO, GDPR compliance, 99.9% uptime SLA

---

## Operations Director

**Role:** Leader managing multiple teams and programmes
**Primary concern:** Efficiency, cost per dossier, team productivity
**Pain point:** Difficulty scaling operations during volume spikes
**How iverif.io helps:** Auto-scaling processing, queue management, productivity metrics

---

## Small Operator (SME)

**Role:** Owner of a small energy services firm processing 50-200 dossiers/year
**Primary concern:** Cost-effectiveness and ease of use
**Pain point:** Cannot afford dedicated compliance staff
**How iverif.io helps:** Pay-per-dossier pricing, managed onboarding, no IT required
