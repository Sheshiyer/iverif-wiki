# Primary Persona: The Energy Subsidy Operator

## Profile

**Name:** Marie Durand (archetype)
**Role:** Operations Manager at a mid-sized energy services company
**Location:** France (also applicable to Germany, UK, other EU markets)
**Company size:** 50-500 employees
**Annual dossier volume:** 500-5,000 per year

---

## Daily Reality

Marie manages a team of 8 operators who process CEE (Certificats d'Economies d'Energie) dossiers. Each dossier requires 10-20 supporting documents — invoices, attestation forms, technical reports, photos — that must cross-check against programme-specific rules.

**Current pain points:**
- Peak periods (Q4) create 3-month backlogs
- 15-20% of dossiers are rejected due to documentation errors
- Regulatory audits are stressful because documentation trails are incomplete
- Training new operators takes 3 months before they are productive

---

## Goals

1. **Reduce rejection rate** from 20% to under 5%
2. **Process dossiers faster** — current average is 4 hours, target is 30 minutes
3. **Pass regulatory audits** without stress or findings
4. **Scale operations** without proportional hiring

---

## Fears

- Regulatory fines for non-compliance
- Losing accreditation as an approved operator
- Reputation damage with prime contractors
- Staff burnout during peak periods

---

## Decision Criteria

When evaluating solutions, Marie prioritizes:
1. **Accuracy** — Will it actually catch errors?
2. **Speed to value** — How long until we see results?
3. **Regulatory acceptance** — Will auditors trust the output?
4. **Ease of integration** — Can my team use it without IT support?

---

## Quote

> *"We process thousands of dossiers per year. One missed document can cost us the entire subsidy. I need a system that catches everything, every time."*
