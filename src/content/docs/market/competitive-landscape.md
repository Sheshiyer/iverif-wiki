# Competitive Landscape

## Market Position

iverif.io operates in the intersection of document management, regulatory compliance, and energy sector software. The competitive landscape includes:

| Category | Examples | iverif.io Differentiation |
|----------|----------|---------------------------|
| Legacy document management | SharePoint, Documentum | Domain-specific rules, not generic storage |
| Generic OCR/AI platforms | Abbyy, Amazon Textract | Pre-configured for EU energy subsidies |
| Compliance software | MetricStream, Thomson Reuters | Purpose-built for dossier validation |
| Energy sector tools | Energy management platforms | Document validation, not monitoring |

---

## Key Differentiators

### 1. Domain-Specific Intelligence
Unlike generic document processing platforms, iverif.io understands EU energy subsidy programme rules natively. No custom rule building required.

### 2. Pre-Configured Rules
CEE, BEG, ECO4 rules are embedded out-of-the-box. Competitors require months of configuration.

### 3. Managed Expertise
iverif.io provides human implementation specialists, not just software. Most competitors offer only technical support.

### 4. Audit-Ready by Design
Complete timestamped audit trails are core to the architecture, not an afterthought.

---

## Competitive Response Strategy

**When compared to legacy document management:**
iverif.io validates documents, not just stores them. It catches errors before submission.

**When compared to generic AI platforms:**
iverif.io understands energy subsidy regulations. Generic OCR cannot check cross-document consistency against programme rules.

**When compared to compliance software:**
iverif.io is purpose-built for energy subsidy dossiers, not a horizontal compliance tool.

---

## Market Opportunity

The EU energy subsidy market processes millions of dossiers annually:
- **CEE (France):** ~2 million operations/year
- **BEG (Germany):** ~1.5 million applications/year
- **ECO4 (UK):** ~500,000 measures/year

With rejection rates of 15-20% due to documentation errors, the addressable market for validation automation is substantial.
