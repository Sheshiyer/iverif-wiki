# Visual Guidelines

## Design Philosophy

**Precision meets regulatory authority.**

The visual system communicates trust and technical competence without visual noise. Every element serves a functional purpose — decoration is avoided, clarity is paramount.

---

## Color System

### Primary: Signal Green
**#2EE600**

Validation, go-signal, correctness. Triggers confidence and action. Used sparingly for maximum impact on CTAs and status indicators.

### Secondary: Regulatory Stone
**#1C1917**

Authority, permanence, seriousness. Anchors the brand in regulatory gravitas without being cold.

### Accent: Clean White
**#FAFAF9**

Clarity, space to think, professionalism. Creates breathing room in dense regulatory interfaces.

### Usage Rules
- Green on white for primary actions and success states
- Stone on white for body text and headings
- White on stone for dark sections or footers
- Green + stone never compete for attention on the same element

---

## Typography

### Headers: Geist Sans Light / SemiBold
Clean, technical, modern. Conveys precision without decoration.

### Body: Geist Sans Regular / Medium
Highly readable, neutral, gets out of the way of content.

### Data: Geist Mono Regular / Medium
Monospace for document fields, validation reports, audit trails. Signals technical accuracy.

---

## Logo Usage

### Clear Space
Minimum 1x icon height on all sides. Never crowd the logo.

### Variations
- **Full wordmark with icon** — Primary usage
- **Icon only** — Favicon, app icon, small spaces
- **White version** — For dark backgrounds

### Constraints
- Never distort or stretch
- Never add effects (shadows, glows, gradients)
- Never place on busy or photographic backgrounds
- Always maintain contrast against background

---

## Imagery & Art Direction

### Photography
Documentary precision. Real regulatory documents, real desks, real processes. No stock-photo humans.

### Illustration
Technical diagrams with Swiss precision. Clean lines, minimal color, information-first.

### Environments
Modern office, regulatory desk, clean workspace. Neutral tones with green accent lighting.

---

## Graphic Elements

### Shapes
Sharp corners, precise angles, grid-aligned. No rounded corners on data elements.

### Textures
Fine-grain stone paper, brushed aluminum, circuit traces — subtle, never dominant.

### Patterns
Grid systems, document flow diagrams, validation checkmark motifs.
