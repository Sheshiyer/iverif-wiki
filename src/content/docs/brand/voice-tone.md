# Voice & Tone

## Brand Persona

**The Compliance Expert**

A seasoned professional with deep knowledge of EU energy subsidy regulations. Speaks with authority but remains accessible — like a trusted colleague who understands the complexities of regulatory compliance and is here to make your job easier.

---

## Voice Attributes

| Attribute | Expression |
|-----------|------------|
| **Precise** | Exact terminology, no ambiguity. "Validation failed: attestation date expired" not "Something looks off." |
| **Authoritative** | Confident expertise without arrogance. We know the regulations so you don't have to worry. |
| **Confident** | Clear statements of capability. "Reduces errors by up to 90%" not "Can help reduce errors." |

---

## Tone by Context

### Product Interface
- Direct and functional
- Error messages explain what happened and how to fix it
- Success states are clear but not celebratory

### Marketing Content
- Problem-aware, not fear-mongering
- Emphasizes outcomes over features
- Uses concrete numbers and timeframes

### Support Communication
- Empathetic to regulatory pressure
- Patient with complexity
- Proactive with solutions

---

## Language Principles

### Do
- Use regulatory terminology correctly (CEE, BEG, ECO4)
- Speak to outcomes: "pass audits" not "comply with regulations"
- Use active voice: "We validate documents" not "Documents are validated"
- Be specific: "4-hour processing time" not "fast processing"

### Don't
- Use hype or superlatives ("revolutionary," "game-changing")
- Be casual or playful with compliance topics
- Use jargon without context
- Make promises we cannot quantify

---

## Empathy Statements

When users encounter issues:
- "We understand the pressure to ensure regulatory compliance."
- "We know how frustrating document errors can be."
- "We're here to help you streamline your validation process."

---

## Emotional Targets

iverif.io communications should evoke:
- **Confidence** — "This will work correctly."
- **Trust** — "They understand my domain."
- **Relief** — "I don't have to worry about missing something."
- **Control** — "I can see exactly what happened."
